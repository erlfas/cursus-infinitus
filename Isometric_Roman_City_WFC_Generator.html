How Roman WFC Works

Socket Symmetry · MRV Heuristic · Domino Propagation
01 / SUPERPOSITION & ENTROPY

Every Cell Begins Uncertain

Initially, each tile in the city grid holds all 68 oriented Roman variants simultaneously. The solver computes weighted Shannon entropy H per cell—fewer compatible tiles produce lower entropy (shown in zellige emerald).

02 / COLLAPSE & PROPAGATION

Domino Constraint Cascades

Each step picks the lowest-entropy cell, collapses it to one weighted tile (with spatial affinity for forums, gardens, and straight roads), and strips incompatible sockets from neighbors using fast bitwise masks.

03 / SOCKET GRAMMAR & MUSEUM

Four Edge Languages

Every module declares four directional sockets: R (Via Romana), A (Aqueduct Specus), F (Forum Plaza), and U (Urban Margin). Switch to Tile Museum to inspect every procedural mesh and socket side-by-side.

Engine concepts adapted from procedural WebGL Wave Function Collapse architecture (inspired by Arcanum: a WFC magic school and Wawa Sensei’s WFC design principles: bitset propagation, socket symmetry, affinity weighting, and side-by-side module museums). All 3D Roman geometry is procedurally synthesized in-browser via Three.js.
